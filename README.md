# Solar-Panel-Cleaner
Engineering project for automated solar panel cleaning using linear motion and Arduino control.


## Overview
A low-cost solar panel cleaning system designed to move along the solar panel frame and clean the panel surface automatically.

## Problem
Dust accumulation on solar panels reduces efficiency and requires regular manual cleaning.

## Solution
A linear-motion cleaning mechanism mounted on the solar panel frame that travels across the panel surface and removes dust.

## Components
- Arduino Uno
- DC Motor
- Motor Driver
- Battery
- Cleaning Brush
- Linear Motion Mechanism
- Frame Mounts

## Working Principle
The cleaning unit is mounted on both ends of the solar panel frame. A motor-driven linear mechanism moves the cleaner from one side of the panel to the other while the brush removes dust from the surface.
